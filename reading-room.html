Reading room

Read and download the full text

These works were all published between 1873 and 1927 and are in the public domain. The PDFs are hosted on this site. Open one in your browser, or save a copy to keep.

Scans keep the marks of earlier readers, such as library stamps, shelf numbers and handwriting. We have not altered them. Some text may be hard to read where a page was torn or stained. More volumes will be added as we check each scan page by page.

Waymark Project and its first program, the Bengal Heritage Archive, are an independent, volunteer-run project; incorporation in progress. It is not a registered nonprofit and does not accept donations.

Book scans come from the Internet Archive, the Digital Library of India and Project Gutenberg. Catalog descriptions are our own and may be reused with credit.
