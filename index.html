Waymark Project

Our first program: Bengal Heritage Archive · বেঙ্গল হেরিটেজ আর্কাইভ

Waymark Project helps families and communities find and keep their history. The Bengal Heritage Archive, its first program, is a small, careful collection of Bengal's written past: full-text books you can read and download here, a catalog of district gazetteers, censuses and ballads held in free digital libraries, family records kept with the family's consent, and a plain guide for diaspora Bangladeshis sorting out birth, NID and land papers.

Start here

Three ways in

I want to find my family's village

  1. Find your district on the map, or take the district quiz.
  2. Open that district's gazetteer. Read the chapters on the people, the land and the towns.
  3. Check the 1901 and 1911 censuses for population, religion and language near you.
  4. Learn the old land words in the glossary.

I want to request a family record

  1. Read the records guide to see what birth, ID and land records contain.
  2. Use the glossary to read terms on an old deed or khatian.
  3. Go to the official office or website the guide names. We cannot handle applications or documents for you.

I want to read the history

  1. Skim the timeline for the frame.
  2. Start with the starred books in the catalog.
  3. Read what we host in the reading room.
  4. Read the card's context note before you trust a colonial source.
